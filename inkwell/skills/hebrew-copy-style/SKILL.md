# Hebrew Copy Style: Quick Checklist

**What this skill is:** a short review pass you run over Hebrew marketing text, either existing or freshly written. It does not write full copy. For full writing by channel (email, WhatsApp, SEO blog, ads, review replies) use the `inkwell` agent from this same plugin.

**Inputs:** the Hebrew text to check, plus the channel if known (email, ad, social post).
**Output:** the corrected text and a one-line note per rule that changed something.

## The seven-question check
1. **Long dash (—)?** Replace it with a plain hyphen "-" or an arrow. A long dash breaks text direction in right-to-left (RTL) text and signals machine-written copy.
2. **Generic transition opener?** Delete phrases such as "furthermore", "it is important to note", "in today's fast-paced world" (Hebrew originals: "יתרה מזאת", "חשוב לציין", "בעולם המהיר של היום").
3. **Inflated adjectives?** ("revolutionary", "amazing") Replace each with a concrete detail.
4. **Conclusion first?** Within one or two sentences the reader must know what this is and what is being asked of them.
5. **One CTA (call to action), specific and visible?** Good: "book a short consultation call". Weak: "get in touch".
6. **Any fact the user did not supply?** Prices, percentages, client counts, testimonials: either a source exists or write `[placeholder]`. Never invent.
7. **Uniform sentence length, lists of exactly three?** Mix short and long sentences; vary list sizes.

## RTL basics
- Parentheses inside a Hebrew sentence open in Hebrew order. Do not attach a bracket directly to a foreign word or number without a space.
- An English paragraph inside a Hebrew document is its own paragraph, never mid-sentence.
- Emoji: zero by default. One at most on WhatsApp or Instagram.
- Dates and numbers follow Israeli conventions (day/month/year).
