# AEO lessons from a real audit (Nov 2026)
1. Before any AEO work, compare the tracked AI prompts and the brief's topics with what the site actually sells. A tracker full of prompts about products the business does not offer reports 0% visibility and tells you nothing. Fix the prompt set first.
2. Do not write pages for services the business cannot prove. No case, no price, no page.
3. Pull real Search Console and GA4 data before judging demand. If a connector is missing, say "unavailable". Do not estimate.
4. Check GA4 key events against sessions. Events above sessions mean the event setup is wrong; do not use them as a lead KPI.
5. Answer-first blocks (3 sentences: what, how, where, with a price range) under the H1 are what engines quote. Cost-range tables and dated "updated" lines help.
6. Traffic often comes from informational posts. Add a contextual path from those posts to service pages.
7. Small, low-authority sites are cited by AI engines for local service prompts, so a specific page can win without many backlinks.
